I'm Gordan Djordjević, a student at the School of Electrical Engineering, University of Belgrade, studying Computer Engineering and Informatics.

What pulls me in is how things work underneath: operating systems, compilers, and code that has to be fast. Recently that meant writing a multithreaded kernel for RISC-V and a compiler for the MikroJava language, and before that, graph algorithms and a full-stack booking platform.

Outside of coursework I like graphics programming with Vulkan and Three.js, tinkering with Arduino, and security — at the 201:GO! hackathon our team built a browser extension that catches phishing emails.

Away from the keyboard: music, and the hi-fi gear to play it on properly. And games, both playing them and making them (I wrote a Brick Breaker game in Java), and the background of this site is a small Game of Life you can draw on with your mouse.
