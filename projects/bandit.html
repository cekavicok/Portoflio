A browser extension that detects phishing emails and explains why a message looks dangerous.

In Catan, when you roll a 7, the Bandit moves and someone loses their resources. Here the Bandit moves onto the scammers.

What it does

With one click, Bandit analyses the email that is open in the browser. It looks at the sender address, subject line, body, links, attachments and the structure of the message, and returns a risk score from 0 to 100% together with the reasons for it. Above 50%, it suggests moving the message to spam.

Each result comes with a short explanation of the trick that was detected, so the user learns what to look for next time instead of only getting a warning.

Where it is going

The wider idea is an education platform around the extension: lessons on phishing tactics and local scam trends, short quizzes, a personal security score, and an admin panel that lets organisations run simulated phishing campaigns and measure how their team responds.

Try it

Clone the repository, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer Mode and choose Load unpacked on the cloned folder.
