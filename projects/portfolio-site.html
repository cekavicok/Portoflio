A portfolio built from plain HTML pages, with no framework and nothing loaded from anywhere else.

Why

I wanted a site that loads instantly, is easy to read, and that I fully understand. Every page is a hand-written HTML file; Vite only bundles the styles and the one script, and its output works from any folder or straight off the disk.

The background

Move the mouse (or touch the screen) and you seed cells of Conway's Game of Life, which then evolve under the standard B3/S23 rules. The animation is written to cost nothing when nothing is happening:

  • When the board empties or settles into still lifes, the loop stops until the next touch.
  • Only the area around live cells is computed, not the whole screen.
  • Only changed pixels are redrawn.
  • It respects prefers-reduced-motion and pauses in background tabs.

On a 1920×1080 screen this brought one generation from 1.24 ms down to 0.03 ms, about 40× faster.

Privacy

No analytics, no cookies, no third-party fonts or scripts. A Content-Security-Policy makes the browser refuse anything not served from the site itself, and outgoing links do not send a referrer.
